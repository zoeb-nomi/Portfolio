## 2. The approach: one spec, many renders

The Decisions API playbook's two lessons drive this: visual concept rounds cost more than the eval, and subagents should return counts and paths, not prose. So:

- **Every post is one file**: `content/posts/pNN.json` holding body, first comment with UTM, X version, alt text, status, fact-check pointers, and a figure spec (primitive plus data). The schedule document, the phone HTML page, the images and the checks are all generated from these files. No hand-maintained final document.
- **One renderer**, `scripts/content/render.mjs`, built on the pattern already in `scripts/generate-og.mjs` (inline HTML, base64 woff2 fonts, Playwright screenshot). Chromium and ffmpeg are already on this machine. It reads tokens.css for colours and fonts, so the images cannot drift from the site. Outputs: PNG 1080x1350; PDF carousels via page.pdf; MP4 and GIF via frame capture plus ffmpeg, with a seamless loop and a first frame that works as a still.
- **Six figure primitives**, each mirroring a site component so posts and pages look like one system: unit strip (squares, like UnitChart), slope or dumbbell, bar list, waterfall, stat tile, and a two-column comparison table. A post's figure is a primitive plus data, chosen in one line of text before anything renders. One render, one contact-sheet review of six thumbnails at a time, fix, done. No concept rounds.
- **A deterministic checker**, `scripts/content/check.mjs`, run before any post is marked READY: no em or en dashes, no emoji, no URL in a LinkedIn body, body 1,200 to 2,500 characters, banned-phrase list, the privacy regexes (phone, personal email, TradeFrame tickers, the word Instead outside the one allowed sentence), alt text present, image exists and is under 5 MB, UTM tag matches the post number. Code does the sweeps, not a model.
- **Separation of duties**: I write copy and choose figures. A separate Sonnet verifier, given only the cloned repos and the live site, checks every number, model name, denominator and attribution in batches of five posts and writes file-and-line pointers into FACT CHECK. A post that fails goes back to me with the diff. The 8 Oct check found 22 issues in 10 posts; I expect the same.
- **Splitting, not inventing, gets us to 25+.** The overloaded posts already contain the extra findings: ZoomInfo 0 to 74 comes out of Post 2; the verdict split (reject 136 of 233 vs hold 157 of 232) comes out of Post 3; judge-vs-judge 117 of 332 comes out of Post 7; the P5 taxonomy miss comes out of Post 8. The Decisions work supports three posts on its own (the 0.9 threshold, 21 of 94 hearsay refusals, the 242 near-zero items of which 17% were yes). Posts 11 to 18 from the lineup plus these splits give 27 to 29 without new research.
